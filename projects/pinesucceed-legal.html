PineSucceed Legal — Legal Matter Operations on Salesforce.

An end-to-end legal matter management solution that turns Salesforce into the operational platform for legal work — from client intake, triage, conflict checks, and matter management through time tracking, agreements, billing, vendor invoices, and reporting.

The Context

Legal work isn't just a case — it's an operation.

Client information, intake documents, conflict checks, matter activity, time entries and billing context are often spread across disconnected tools. PineSucceed Legal makes Salesforce the single operational platform for legal work.

⚠️

The Challenge

  • Client intake, documents, and case activity scattered across emails, drives, and spreadsheets
  • Conflict checks performed informally — with no persisted record that they happened
  • Time entries captured in one tool, billing produced in another, with no clean link between them
  • Vendor invoices tracked outside the system, disconnected from the matters they relate to
  • No single view of active matters, workload, or billing across the firm
✓

Our Solution

  • Structured Intake across General, In-House Legal Request, and Personal Injury types — with dedicated PI questionnaire
  • Persisted Conflict Checks on the Intake and re-runnable on the Matter, forming a durable audit trail
  • Intake-to-Matter conversion that carries context forward and prevents duplicate matters
  • Matter management with stage progression, auto-generated tasks, team-based sharing, and involved parties
  • Time Tracking (live timer + manual entries) feeding directly into Client Bills, with separate Vendor Invoice handling

Walkthrough

Inside legal operations.

Ten screens covering the full legal matter lifecycle — matter workspace, time tracking, tasks, agreements, client billing, intake with conflict checks, vendor invoices, and reporting.

01
Matter Workspace Personal Injury matter with stage bar (Investigation → Treatment → Demand → Negotiation → Settlement → Trial), matter information, financials, and activity feed with tasks like Run Conflict Check, Schedule Initial Consultation, and Open Client File.
02
Time Entries Structured time entry list with billable flag, description, category (Correspondence / Discovery / Document Review), amount, discount, and amount after discount — feeding the matter's billable totals.
03
Live Time Tracking Timer component on the Matter with Start / Pause / Log Time — capturing work as it happens and posting straight into the matter's time entries.
04
Tasks & Task Sets Open Tasks list showing Matter-linked activities (Run Conflict Check, Open Client File, Schedule Initial Consultation), with the Task detail view showing a Task Set — Discovery Checklist in use.
05
Retainer & Engagement Agreement Generated agreement document with scope of representation, contingency fee tiers (33⅓% / 40% / 45%), client responsibilities, termination, and governing law — tailored to the matter type.
06
Client Bill Invoice generated from recorded time entries — line items with date, description, hours, rate, and amount, with fee total and net total, plus terms of payment.
07
Intake Management Intake with a stage bar reflecting the retainer lifecycle (Retainer Agreement Sent → Signed → Retained → Turned Down), core info, request classification, contact detail, and linked matter — plus files and Intake History.
08
Conflict Checks — Persisted Four persisted Conflict Check records (CC-00001 through CC-00004) on the Intake's Related tab — creating a durable audit trail instead of a one-time screen result.
09
Vendor Invoice Inbound invoice from outside counsel (Whitfield & Cole LLP) with a lifecycle status bar (Under Review → Sent to A/P → Paid), matter linkage, and invoice PDF generation — tracking money the firm owes, separately from client billing.
10
Operations Dashboard Task Performance view across matters and assignees, Tasks by Status, Intakes by Status, and a Matters by Outcome gauge — giving the firm visibility into workload, intake flow, and case outcomes.
What we built

Key capabilities.

Salesforce as the operational platform for legal work — from first client contact through matter close-out, billing, and reporting.

📥

Intake Management

Structured Intake across General, In-House Legal Request, and Personal Injury types — capturing client, legal request, case type, and history before a Matter is opened.

📋

Triage & PI Questionnaire

Dedicated triage process, with a Personal Injury questionnaire capturing incident, injury, treatment, and other case-specific context directly on the Intake.

🛡️

Conflict Checks

Conflict Checks persisted as records on the Intake and re-runnable from the Matter — establishing an audit trail, not just a one-time screen result.

🔄

Intake-to-Matter Conversion

Conversion that carries client, description, and case type from the Intake into the Matter, with duplicate prevention so the same intake can't be converted twice.

⚖️

Matter Management

The central workspace for legal engagements — matter stages, client info, case details, tasks, time entries, documents, team, involved parties, and commercial fields in one record.

✅

Tasks & Task Sets

Auto-generated tasks at Matter creation (Schedule Initial Consultation, Open Client File, Conflict Check) plus Task Sets to organize repeatable legal workflows.

⏱️

Time Tracking

Both timer-based and manual time entry — capturing billable work as it happens or after the fact, with categories and discounts feeding into amounts.

👥

Matter Team & Involved Parties

Matter Team members with programmatic sharing so access is scoped to the engagement, plus Involved Parties beyond the client — supporting conflict checking and case context.

📄

Agreement Generation

Engagement agreements generated for the matter — supporting models like contingency and hourly, tailored to the type of legal engagement.

💵

Client Billing

Client Bills created from recorded time entries, with statuses covering Sent, Paid, Partially Paid, and Written Off — connecting legal work to what the client pays.

📑

Vendor Invoice Management

Vendor invoices tracked separately from client billing — capturing external invoices (expert, co-counsel) with line items, review status, and payment lifecycle.

📊

Reporting & Dashboards

Visibility into active matters, matter stages, workload, tasks, time entries, and billing — sitting on the same records the legal team works from daily.
