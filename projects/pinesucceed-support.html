PineSucceed Support — a connected customer-service platform.

A Salesforce-based customer service platform connecting self-service, case management, automated customer identification, entitlement-driven SLAs, queue routing, Omni-Channel, Knowledge and operational reporting — from request intake through resolution.

The Context

Support at scale needs more than a case list.

Enterprise support teams juggle self-service, customer identification, service commitments, agent routing and reporting — often across disconnected tools. PineSucceed Support unifies the entire support lifecycle inside Salesforce.

⚠️

The Challenge

  • Support requests arriving through scattered channels with no single system of record
  • Unidentified customers consuming agent time and skewing reporting
  • Service commitments (First Response, Resolution) tracked manually or missed entirely
  • No visibility into which cases are approaching or breaching SLA
  • Manual case assignment slowing down response times
✓

Our Solution

  • Experience Cloud self-service portal for registered and guest customers
  • Automated customer identification via Email-to-Case matching against Salesforce Contacts
  • Entitlement-driven SLA tracking with Standard, Enterprise & Premium tiers
  • Custom SLA Health LWC surfacing real-time First Response & Resolution status
  • Queue + Omni-Channel routing delivering work to available agents in the Service Console

Walkthrough

Inside the platform.

Key screens from the customer portal and the agent's Service Console — the two halves of the support experience.

01
Customer Self-Service Portal Experience Cloud home — request submission, request tracking, and knowledge search for registered customers, with profile context pulled from the Salesforce Contact.
02
Submit a Request Guided intake form with subject, category/subcategory, and description — creating a Salesforce Case directly from the portal.
03
Request Confirmation Post-submission confirmation screen with the generated case number — closing the loop for the customer immediately.
04
SLA & Operational Dashboard Management view of open cases, high-priority load, SLA-at-risk and SLA-breached counts, SLA health by milestone, support-tier breakdown, and the case queue requiring attention.
05
Case Console — SLA Health + Knowledge + Customer 360 Agent view combining the custom SLA Health LWC (First Response & Resolution with warning/breach states), Knowledge suggestions, and a Customer 360 context panel in one screen.
06
Omni-Channel + Queue-Based Routing Live Omni-Channel work items delivered to available agents in the Service Console, with case detail, SLA status, and Jira engineering escalation context on the same screen.
07
Salesforce Knowledge Published support articles (e.g. troubleshooting API authentication errors) served to agents and customers for self-service resolution.
08
Jira Engineering Escalation Cases escalated to Jira for engineering follow-up — Salesforce remains the customer-service system of record while Jira handles engineering workflow.
09
Cross-CRM Integration View HubSpot (marketing context), Zoho CRM (sales context), and Jira (engineering) synchronized into Salesforce — giving agents full customer lifecycle visibility inside the Service Console.
What we built

Key capabilities.

Delivered using a Native → Flow → Apex → LWC approach, so configuration does the heavy lifting and custom code is reserved for where it adds real value.

🌐

Experience Cloud Portal

Self-service portal for registered customers and guest scenarios — submit, track, and search knowledge without agent involvement.

🔍

Automated Customer Identification

Email-to-Case senders matched against Salesforce Contacts; valid customers get their Account/service context, unmatched ones fall into a Guest support flow.

⏱️

Entitlements & Milestones

Standard, Enterprise and Premium support tiers with First Response and Resolution milestones driving SLA commitments per customer.

📊

Custom SLA Health LWC

Real-time SLA visibility on the Case — entitlement tier, overall health, First Response and Resolution status with warning and breach states.

🔀

Queue + Omni-Channel Routing

Cases distributed through queues and Omni-Channel to available agents in the Service Console — no manual assignment required.

👤

Customer 360 Component

Agent-side contextual panel surfacing account, contact, support tier, and service snapshot directly on the Case record.

📚

Salesforce Knowledge

Reusable support articles served to customers and suggested to agents on the Case — reducing resolution time on known issues.

📈

Reports & Dashboards

Management visibility into cases, SLA performance, support-tier distribution, and operational workload.

🔗

Multi-System Lifecycle

HubSpot for marketing context, Zoho CRM for sales context, Jira for engineering escalation — unified inside Salesforce Service.
